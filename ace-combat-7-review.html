Ace Combat 7: Skies Unknown

Score: 8.8 / 10

Genre: Action • Arcade

Review

Ace Combat 7 is personally one of my favorite games of all time. The story, even if its a bit basic, is engaging. The gameplay isn't super realistic, but if you're looking for a fun arcade-like flight game, this might be for you. The game doesn't fail in the slightest in making you feel like an ace pilot. The graphics are really good, and there's a wide variety of planes to choose from. There are also quite a few easter eggs/different ways of playing the missions! This made trying to 100% the game an interesting, yet very challenging experience. I personally wouldn't label this as a flight simulator, as it's pretty unrealistic.

Final Verdict: One of the best arcade "flight sims" out there.
