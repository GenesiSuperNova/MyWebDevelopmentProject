Gang Beasts

Score: 8.9 / 10

Genre: Party • Multiplayer

Review

Gang Beasts is one of best party games out there. It's a really fun experience playing with friends, and is almost always pure laughs. The character controls are extremely floppy and all over the place, which makes for some really funny moments. The simple design and theme of the game is very easy on the eyes and

Pros

  • Incredibly fun with friends
  • Endless replayability
  • Perfect party game

Cons

  • Can get frustrating with random online players
  • A little buggy(can lead to really fun moments)
  • Simple graphics (but charming)

Final Verdict: Buy this game if you have friends. 10/10 party experience.
